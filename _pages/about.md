I am a master's student in Computational Data Science at **Carnegie Mellon University**. At CMU's
Language Technologies Institute I lead two projects: pre-training data curation with
[Chenyan Xiong](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~cx/) ([ReScraper](https://cxcscmu.github.io/ReScraper/)) and
long-horizon auto-research agents with [Yiming Yang](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~yiming/)
([TraceML](https://jerryyan123.github.io/TraceML/), NeurIPS 2026). I have also worked on agentic systems
with [William W. Cohen](https://wwcohen.github.io/) (sponsored by Google DeepMind) and on predicting the
RL utility of training environments with [Sanmi Koyejo](https://cs.stanford.edu/~sanmi/) at **Stanford**.

My research asks **what actually makes a language model better**: which data is worth training on,
which environments make RL pay off, and why agents still fail on long-horizon work.

This summer I was a software engineer intern at **Google**, building AI agents. I graduated
*magna cum laude* from the **University of Washington**. I am looking for **research scientist / engineer**
roles starting 2027.

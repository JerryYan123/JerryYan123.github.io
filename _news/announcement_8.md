*TraceML* was accepted to the **NeurIPS 2026** Evaluations & Datasets Track and will also appear at the NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Evaluation of Interactive Agents. See the [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26086), the [project page](https://jerryyan123.github.io/TraceML/), and the [open-source toolkit](https://github.com/JerryYan123/TraceML) for reading any ML-agent run against human Kaggle practice.
